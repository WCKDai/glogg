# Code of conduct

glogg follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing someone's private information are not welcome in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

Report problems to **boy@wckd.cc**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban people who break these rules, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
